skosdex

All the openly-licensed SKOS in the world — fetched, normalized, canonicalized, indexed.

skosdex collects SKOS concept schemes (thesauri, subject headings, classification schemes, code lists), normalizes them to canonical N-Quads (RDFC-1.0), and serves the openly-licensed corpus over SPARQL and full-text search, with semantic embeddings on top.

  • Live demo & concept browser search, concept cards, embedding galaxies — skosdex.fly.dev
  • SPARQL query cookbook Oxigraph endpoint, cross-scheme mappings, federation to Wikidata/DBpedia
  • Corpus manifest every bundled scheme with its explicit license and quad count
  • About SKOS a short history of the Simple Knowledge Organization System
  • Source repository pipeline, per-scheme metadata, and canonical data (Git LFS)

Licensing: only explicitly openly-licensed data is redistributed here; share-alike and non-commercial schemes are served (unmodified, with their licenses) from the live endpoints only. See the manifest for details.
