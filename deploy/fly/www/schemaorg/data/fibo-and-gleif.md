# FIBO financial vocabulary and GLEIF

## 2016-05-04 — Financial vocabulary integrated

Category: Non-Google
Status: Released (v3.0)
Summary: **FIBO and its W3C community** contribute financial-product terminology, including monetary amounts and compound prices.

The persistent acknowledgement identifies the Financial Industry Business Ontology project as the source for numerous finance definitions. Richard Wallis applied the financial changes in the repository.

Sources:

- [FIBO acknowledgement](https://github.com/schemaorg/schemaorg/blob/main/data/collab/FIBO.md)
- [Integration commit](https://github.com/schemaorg/schemaorg/commit/27117ca8)
- [Release record](https://schema.org/docs/releases.html#v3.0)

## 2016-10-26 — GLEIF support explicitly acknowledged

Category: Non-Google
Status: Attribution recorded
Summary: **GLEIF's support for Legal Entity Identifiers** is acknowledged on `leiCode` in a commit by Richard Wallis.
