# BBC, EBU and sports vocabulary

## 2013-11-21 — TV and radio vocabulary integrated

Category: Non-Google
Status: Released (v1.0d)
Summary: **Yves Raimond (BBC) and Jean-Pierre Evain (EBU)** lead TV/radio improvements, working with Dan Brickley on integration.

The design builds on TV-Anytime, EBUCore and the BBC Programmes Ontology, with explicit equivalence links. It covers series, seasons, episodes, clips, broadcasts and on-demand availability. Release: November 21; explanatory guest post: December 3.

Sources:

- [BBC guest post and ontology lineage](https://blog.schema.org/2013/12/03/schema-org-for-tv-and-radio-markup/)
- [Release announcement](https://blog.schema.org/2013/11/21/schema-org-v1-0d-published-tv-radio-civic-services-contactpoint-event-and-organization-improvements/)
- [W3C TV/radio proposal](https://www.w3.org/wiki/WebSchemas/TVRadioSchema)
