# Automotive Ontology Working Group

## 2015-05-12 — Automotive vocabulary integrated

Category: Non-Google
Status: Released (v2.0)
Summary: **Martin Hepp, Mirek Sopek, Karol Szczepański and the Automotive Ontology community** contribute detailed vehicle descriptions.

The work adds such concepts as engine specifications, fuel consumption, efficiency and vehicle configuration. The release announcement explicitly names the community contributors.

Sources:

- [Announcement and credits](https://blog.schema.org/2015/05/13/schema-org-2-0/)
- [Persistent acknowledgement](https://github.com/schemaorg/schemaorg/blob/main/data/collab/Automotive_Ontology_Working_Group.md)

## 2015-08-06 — Automotive hosted extension

Category: Non-Google
Status: Hosted extension preview (v2.1)
Summary: **The Automotive Ontology community** extends its contribution through the `auto.schema.org` hosted vocabulary.

Offered for final review in v2.1; finalized in v3.0 on May 4, 2016.
