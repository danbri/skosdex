# Tourism community and IIT-CNR

## 2017-08-14 — Tourist attractions and public access

Category: Non-Google
Status: Released (v3.3)
Summary: **The Tourism Structured Web Data community and IIT-CNR, Pisa** inform richer tourist-attraction, language and public-access descriptions.

TouristAttraction and touristType carry acknowledgements of the Future Internet Lab at IIT-CNR and the Tourism group. Richard Wallis integrated the proposal. Credit applies to these acknowledged elements, not automatically every place-related term.

Sources:

- [Proposal #1459](https://github.com/schemaorg/schemaorg/issues/1459)
