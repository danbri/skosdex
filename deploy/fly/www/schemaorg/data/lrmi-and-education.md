## 2020-07-21 — LearningResource added

Category: Non-Google
Status: Released in Pending (v9.0)
Summary: **LRMI-related community work**, integrated by Dan Brickley, adds `LearningResource` as a general type for educational content.

Brickley's July 1 commit explicitly bases the addition on a 2016 design and recent discussion. This records the integration date separately from the longer design history.

Sources:

- [Proposal #1401](https://github.com/schemaorg/schemaorg/issues/1401)
