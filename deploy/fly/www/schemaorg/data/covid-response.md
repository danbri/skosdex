# COVID-19 response and CDC hospital reporting

## 2020-03-17 — Emergency announcements and online events

Category: Project
Status: Released (v7.0; includes Pending additions)
Summary: Schema.org fast-tracks `SpecialAnnouncement` and expands online-event descriptions during the COVID-19 response.

The blog is dated March 16 while the release index records March 17; this entry uses the release date. The change includes VirtualLocation, eventAttendanceMode and EventMovedOnline.

Sources:

- [Release record](https://schema.org/docs/releases.html#v7.0)

## 2020-04-02 — CDC hospital reporting model

Category: Alignment
Status: Released (v7.03)
Summary: **The US CDC's hospital-reporting data format** is represented in Schema.org to support pandemic resource reporting.

The release describes a one-to-one representation of the CDC CSV fields. That establishes the source model, not necessarily direct CDC authorship of the Schema.org definitions. The explanatory announcement followed April 6.

Sources:

- [Proposal #2521](https://github.com/schemaorg/schemaorg/issues/2521)
