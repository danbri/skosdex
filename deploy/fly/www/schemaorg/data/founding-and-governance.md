# Founding and community governance

## 2011-06-02 — Schema.org launches

Category: Non-Google
Status: Project milestone
Summary: **Microsoft/Bing and Yahoo**, together with Google, launch a shared vocabulary for structured web data.

The contemporary July announcement explicitly dates the launch to June 2. Yandex joined later in 2011; it should not be backdated to launch day.

Sources:

- [Contemporary account, July 2011](https://blog.schema.org/page/5/)

## 2011-09-21 — First community workshop

Category: Non-Google
Status: Project milestone
Summary: **W3C, Creative Commons, RDFa, Microformats, publishers and toolmakers** join the first Schema.org workshop.

The September 28 report records a September 21 workshop with 75 attendees, including Ask, Yandex, Baidu, NYTimes, Disney, Foursquare, Shopping.com, OpenTable, Drupal and SharePoint. Participation is credited here as community input, not as authorship of particular terms.

Sources:

- [Workshop report](https://blog.schema.org/2011/09/28/schema-org-workshop-wrap-up/)

## 2015-05-13 — Community Group and steering group

Category: Non-Google
Status: Project milestone
Summary: **W3C and community contributors** gain formal roles through the Schema.org Community Group and public steering-group process.

The v2.0 announcement describes the governance change, public-schemaorg mailing list, GitHub issue tracking and versioned vocabulary snapshots. Vocabulary release date: May 12; governance announcement: May 13.

Sources:

- [Schema.org 2.0 announcement](https://blog.schema.org/2015/05/13/schema-org-2-0/)
