# European Legislation Identifier (ELI)

## 2017-08-14 — Legislation vocabulary

Category: Non-Google
Status: Released in Pending (v3.3)
Summary: **The ELI taskforce and Thomas Francart** contribute legislation descriptions based on the European Legislation Identifier ontology.

The release explicitly credits ELI. Git records Francart's proposal and Dan Brickley's integration work. ELI represents a collaboration of national legislation publishers and European institutions.

## 2025-03-24 — ELI model updates incorporated

Category: Non-Google
Status: Released (v29.0)
Summary: **Thomas Francart's ELI follow-up** adds relationships for amendments, repeals, corrections, commencement and applicability.

The proposal opened in March 2020 and Francart's source changes date from September 2020. The PR was merged in February 2025 and appears in v29.0. This long interval is why proposal, merge and release dates must be distinguished.
