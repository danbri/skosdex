# GoodRelations

## 2012-11-08 — GoodRelations integrated

Category: Non-Google
Status: Released (v0.99)
Summary: **Martin Hepp and the GoodRelations project** contribute a substantial e-commerce model for products, offers, businesses and prices.

Schema.org also reorganized its publishing machinery to accept community RDFa/RDFS definitions. Persistent acknowledgement markup attributes the reused vocabulary to Hepp; a maintainer's integration commit should not replace that design credit.
