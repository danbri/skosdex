## eventAttendanceMode

Property · current · [Schema.org](https://schema.org/eventAttendanceMode)

The eventAttendanceMode of an event indicates whether it occurs online, offline, or a mix.

Date evidence: First observed source declaration; it may precede release and does not establish invention.

## hasDriveThroughService

Property · current · [Schema.org](https://schema.org/hasDriveThroughService)

Indicates whether some facility (e.g. [[FoodEstablishment]], [[CovidTestingFacility]]) offers a service that can be used by driving through in a car. In the case of [[CovidTestingFacility]] such facilities could potentially help with social distancing from other potentially-infected users.

Date evidence: First observed source declaration; it may precede release and does not establish invention.
