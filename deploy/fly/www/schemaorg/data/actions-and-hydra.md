# Actions and the Hydra community

## 2013-08-07 — Actions vocabulary introduced

Category: Project
Status: Released (v1.0c)
Summary: Schema.org adds descriptions of actions already performed, laying the foundation for later invocable actions.

Sources:

- [Release record](https://schema.org/docs/releases.html#v1.0c)

## 2014-04-16 — Potential Actions

Category: Non-Google
Status: Released (v1.2)
Summary: **Hydra and the W3C Web standards community** inform a mechanism for describing actions a website enables and how to invoke them.

Schema.org's acknowledgement explicitly credits extensive discussion with the Hydra community group. This is collaboration credit; it does not mean Schema.org adopted the Hydra vocabulary wholesale.

Sources:

- [Actions announcement](https://blog.schema.org/2014/04/16/announcing-schema-org-actions/)
- [Persistent Hydra acknowledgement](https://github.com/schemaorg/schemaorg/blob/main/data/collab/ActionCollabClass.md)
