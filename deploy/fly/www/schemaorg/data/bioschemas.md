# Bioschemas

## 2021-07-07 — Life-science types integrated

Category: Non-Google
Status: Released in Pending (v13.0)
Summary: **Bioschemas** contributes biological and chemical entities, including genes, proteins, taxa, molecular entities and chemical substances.

The release credits Bioschemas. Richard Wallis merged the terms into Pending in May; July 7 is the numbered release. The addition also includes BioChemEntity and supporting relationships. Bioschemas' own profiles remain independently maintained and should not be confused with Schema.org core status.
