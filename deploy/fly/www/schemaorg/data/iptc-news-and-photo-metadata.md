# IPTC: news, photo metadata and digital provenance

## 2011-09-27 — rNews incorporated

Category: Non-Google
Status: Released
Summary: **IPTC, NYTimes, Associated Press and Getty Images** contribute rNews-based news and publishing properties.

The announcement names Evan Sandhaus (NYTimes), Stuart Myles (AP) and Andreas Gebhard (Getty Images). Additions include article datelines and relationships to print editions. This is vocabulary collaboration, not just publisher adoption.

Sources:

- [Contemporary announcement and credits](https://blog.schema.org/2011/09/27/extended-schema-org-news-support/)
- [Persistent rNews acknowledgement](https://github.com/schemaorg/schemaorg/blob/main/data/collab/rNews.md)

## 2022-02-25 — Photo metadata mapping guidelines

Category: Non-Google
Status: External mapping
Summary: **IPTC and CIPA/Exif experts**, consulting Schema.org, publish mappings between photo metadata standards and Schema.org.

The guidelines' version history dates the initial version to February 25; the public announcement followed March 4. These are external mapping guidelines, not a Schema.org vocabulary release.

Sources:

- [Guidelines and version history](https://www.iptc.org/std/photometadata/documentation/mappingguidelines/)
- [IPTC announcement](https://iptc.org/news/iptc-announces-rules-for-mapping-photo-metadata-between-iptc-exif-and-schema-org-standards/)

## 2024-01-09 — Digital source types, including AI-generated media

Category: Non-Google
Status: Released (v24.0)
Summary: **IPTC's Digital Source Type vocabulary** supplies an enumeration for identifying how media was created, including generative AI.

The release explicitly credits collaboration with IPTC. Dan Brickley drafted the Schema.org representation in October 2023; January 2024 is the numbered-release date.

Sources:

- [Release record](https://schema.org/docs/releases.html#v24.0)
- [Proposal #3392](https://github.com/schemaorg/schemaorg/issues/3392)
